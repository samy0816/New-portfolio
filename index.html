01 About

I like making technology feel less like "technology" and more like something you just get. My background's in Computer Engineering, but along the way I got hooked on the human side of things — why some products feel effortless while others feel like a fight.

That curiosity brought me to Saarland University, where I'm doing my MSc in Informatics, exploring human–computer interaction. Most of my projects start the same way: a vague idea, a pile of data, or a problem that hasn't been solved yet. I enjoy untangling all that, figuring out what really matters, and building something that's clear, quick, and easy to use.
